About Me

Turning Business Data Into Useful Information

I enjoy understanding what business data is saying and turning it into information people can actually use.

I've been working with data for a while, developing practical experience in analysing business information, preparing reports, reconciling records and creating useful Excel and Power BI solutions.

My interest in Data Analytics comes from seeing how properly organised data can help a business move from simply having information to actually understanding what is happening within the business.

I am interested in working with different types of businesses where data can be used to improve performance, understand customers, manage resources and drive better results.

My background in internal auditing has also strengthened my attention to detail, accuracy, reconciliation and understanding of business processes.
